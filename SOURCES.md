# Sources and evidence boundaries

The public spatial baseline is OpenStreetMap data acquired 2026-09-16, ODbL 1.0, with an adapted database offer in every Release. Source CRS EPSG:4326; model CRS EPSG:3414 translated to a local meter origin. Heights absent from OSM were estimated; vegetation and generic appearance are procedural.

Selected source page URLs, date uncertainty, observed direction and supported attributes are in [source-register.json](docs/history/source-register.json). This is a reference index, not a redistribution grant. Photo dates may be unknown; an access date, upload-path year or webpage copyright year is not automatically a capture date. University guides establish identity and relative locations only, not exact footprints, widths or dimensions.

Official baseline references include [NTU campus art trail](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/life-at-ntu/museum/campus-art-trail), [ADM research](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/adm/research), and [ADM roof](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/news/detail/art-blooms-on-iconic-sloping-grass-roof-at-ntu-singapore). No source images/PDFs are supplied. Neither photographs nor estimates are labelled measured or fully verified.

The public register excludes personal uploader names, local paths, private provenance logs and Google account/place metadata. Google-derived later geometry is not released. Public documents preserve copyright limits; they do not guarantee legal clearance for every possible downstream use.
