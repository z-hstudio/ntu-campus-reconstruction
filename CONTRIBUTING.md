# Contributing

Issues, evidence of real-world errors, suggestions and pull requests are welcome. Identify the release, building ID, date, observation direction and what a source actually supports. Distinguish measured, observed, inferred and estimated attributes. Do not upload restricted photos, private information, scans or materials without redistribution rights.

Keep contributions focused and recoverable. Include matching before/after views, stable-ID coverage, coordinate and material checks where geometry changes. Do not substitute object-count checks for geometry validation.

Contributors keep credit for their own additions and must identify the rights and licence of each addition. Submission does not transfer ownership or authorize commercial sublicensing of your additions automatically. Do not add terms that relicense retained original work incompatibly. The original model source credit **Manyousang Z / z-hstudio** must remain, including in extensively modified editions. Contributions cannot convert the whole project to an incompatible licence.
